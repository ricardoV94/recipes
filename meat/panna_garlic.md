# Panna-garlic meat

1. One whole chicken breast, cut in pieces or stripes, grill seasoning, ideally let it stay for 1 day
2. Put oil in pan, roast it for 5 minutes
3. Put a mug of water to cover (not too much or it will be watery!), cover the pan, let it cook for 20 minutes on low heat, until it becomes soft and the water is gone a bit
4. Take it off the fire, put 1 tablespoon of flour, mix it well, put it back to the fire
5. Add 200 ml of panna, ¼ or bit more of garlic cream, cook it together for 5 minutes, add parsley on top
