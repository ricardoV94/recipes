## Carrot meat

1. One full chicken breast (800 g) wrapped in flour → precook, put in buttered glass pot.
2. Cut 1 onion in half moons, 3 carrots, 3 white carrots → precook, maybe add salt (mum doesn’t)
3. Add half a lemon’s juice, 3 dl water, bit of flour
4. Put on top of meat, put it in oven, 220 C, covered.
5. When almost ready, take off cover, add 1 full glass of sour cream on top, cook for 10 more minutes.
