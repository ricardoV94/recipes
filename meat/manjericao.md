# Manjericão meat / Bajor

One whole chicken, add salt and pepper, precook in flour.

Put it in buttered pot.

Cut a small onion in small pieces, cook it on oil + 1 big spoon of tomato puree + 3 dl water + 1 teaspoon of basil + salt.

Boil it, keep stirring. Put on top of meat.

Cook it covered (40 minutes / 1 hour), take off the foil. Add 1 bottle of sour cream + smoked cheese, cook for 10 more minutes until it melts.
