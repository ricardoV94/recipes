## Easy pad thai

### **Ingredients**

**The Sauce (Makes enough for 2 large portions):**
* **Fish sauce:** ~1 tablespoon (or use soy sauce to keep it meatless) [00:01:05]
* **Tamarind concentrate:** ~2 teaspoons [00:01:21]
* **Sugar:** 2-3 tablespoons (white, brown, or less refined sugar works) [00:00:59]
* **Ketchup:** ~2 tablespoons [00:01:51]

**Aromatics & Mix-ins:**
* **Rice noodles:** Flat and narrow (2 oz or 60g per portion) [00:05:22]
* **Chicken breast:** 1 breast, thinly sliced across the grain (can substitute with shrimp or tofu) [00:03:31]
* **Eggs:** 2 eggs, heavily salted and thoroughly beaten [00:03:09]
* **Green onions:** 1 bunch (whites sliced thin for the stir-fry, greens reserved for garnish) [00:02:23]
* **Ginger:** Thumb-sized piece, peeled and chopped [00:02:38]
* **Garlic:** Roughly equal amount to the ginger, peeled and coarsely chopped [00:02:43]
* **Red chili:** 1 chili, thinly sliced [00:02:47]
* **Mung bean sprouts:** 1 large handful per portion [00:03:03]
* **Peanuts:** A big handful, coarsely chopped [00:02:13]
* **Cooking oil:** A splash for stir-frying [00:06:15]
* **Water:** Keep some on hand for deglazing and adjusting the sauce texture [00:03:59]

**Garnishes:**
* Cilantro leaves [00:02:52]
* Lime wedges [00:02:58]
* Reserved green onion tops
* Extra chopped peanuts

---

### **Instructions**

**1. Prep Your Ingredients**
* Stir-fries move extremely fast, so prepare everything before turning on the stove. 
* Salt your beaten eggs heavily and let them sit for about 10 minutes. The salt loosens the proteins and allows them to cook into thin sheets [00:03:09]. 
* Cut the chicken breast into three sections, then slice as thinly as possible *across* the grain for maximum tenderness [00:03:31]. Season the meat with salt right before cooking.

**2. Make the Sauce**
* In a small bowl, combine the fish sauce, tamarind concentrate, sugar, and ketchup [00:00:29]. Leave it thick—do not dilute it with water yet. 

**3. Parboil the Noodles**
* Bring slightly salted water to a boil in a wide pan [00:05:17]. 
* Drop in the rice noodles (break them in half if you prefer them easier to toss) and boil for 2 to 3 minutes maximum [00:05:32]. 
* Drain them in a strainer and shock them with cold water so they stop cooking and don't stick together [00:05:55]. Wipe your pan out to reuse it for the stir-fry.

**4. Stir-Fry (Cook one portion at a time)**
* Place your non-stick pan over high heat and add a little cooking oil [00:06:10]. 
* Once the oil just begins to smoke, drop in a single portion's worth of seasoned chicken in a flat layer and let it sit undisturbed to brown [00:06:25].
* When the bottom two-thirds of the chicken pieces are opaque, toss in half of your aromatics (green onion whites, ginger, garlic, and chili) and stir aggressively [00:06:46].
* Push the chicken and aromatics to one side of the pan. Pour half of the beaten egg mixture into the empty space in a thin layer [00:07:18]. Let it solidify, then scrape it with a spatula to form thin egg sheets. 
* Push the cooked eggs to the side and add a portion of the parboiled noodles [00:07:45]. 
* Add half of your sauce, a big handful of bean sprouts, and some chopped peanuts into the pan [00:07:57]. Stir everything vigorously.
* Once mixed, add a splash of water to the pan [00:08:50]. This deglazes the pan, distributes the thick sauce evenly, and perfectly coats the noodles. Give it a few seconds to tighten up, then remove from heat.

**5. Garnish and Serve**
* Plate the hot Pad Thai and top with cilantro, a squeeze of fresh lime juice, the reserved green onion tops, and extra peanuts for a crunchy texture [00:09:20]. 

*Repeat the cooking process for the second portion.*
