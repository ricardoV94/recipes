# Shrimps with couscous

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZIT4ha9CIVI

## Ingredients
- Olive oil
- 1/2 tbsp of mustard
- 225gr shrimp
- 1 leek
- 1,5 carrots
- 2 big clove garlic
- 250gr steamed couscous
- 200gr peas


## Steps
1. Season shrimps with salt and pepper and fry in olive oil for 2-3 minutes.
2. Add a small amount of mustard and extra olive oil.
3. After 30 seconds add garlic, carrots and leek (and peas if frozen). Cook for 5 minutes.
4. Mix in couscous and cook for another 5 minutes.
